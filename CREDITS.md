# Credits

Maintainer: Finn Karstens.

The transcript extraction follows the domain model and XML parsing work in Finn Karstens's earlier Reden-Portal implementation. The current implementation reworks that approach into a standalone SQLite corpus tool with transactional imports and automated regression tests.

Third-party runtime libraries are SQLite JDBC (Xerial), Jackson and SLF4J. Their respective licenses and notices apply. The source repository does not bundle third-party transcripts or media.

The example sessions are synthetic. Their names, groups and speeches do not describe real parliamentary proceedings.
